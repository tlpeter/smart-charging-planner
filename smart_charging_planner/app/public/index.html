Use a date/time helper (and optionally a number helper for the battery level), for example to set the departure from a dashboard or an automation.

Calendar

Trips in a calendar become departures. The start of the event is the departure time. All-day events are skipped.

A target in the event ("doel: 80", "target: 90" or "85%") is used for that trip. "precondition: ja" is read and shown, but not used yet.

How the app starts and stops charging

Found by the control check above; for example choose the method your own automation used.

Only used for charging on solar surplus (Settings › Solar); otherwise the app only starts and pauses the charger and your load balancer keeps setting the current.

Car

The car's own charge limit follows every choice. The app sets it to the highest goal that is active: your next departure, Charge now, or Ready for tomorrow / the day after. When that goal ends, it goes back. Only while the car is plugged in, and only when the car supports it. A new limit is sent once; it is only sent again if the car still shows the old value after 15 minutes (at most 3 times), because the car's cloud limits the number of calls.

Timing and price

While this entity is "on", the charger stays on, so heating or cooling the car uses grid power instead of the battery. For example the car's HVAC status, or your own helper. Only entities of your car and entities about preconditioning, HVAC or airco are listed.

Always on: once a planned period has started, it is finished even if a new calculation would move it, and a charger status that is briefly invalid (up to 2 minutes) is ignored.

Solar

Charge with your own solar power: the plan counts on the expected sun, and the app charges on the surplus you would otherwise export. On Home you choose how to charge: the price plan, plan and solar, or solar only.

Forecast

Value of your own solar power

From 1 January 2027 "salderen" stops in the Netherlands: a kWh you export earns the feed-in compensation, not what you pay for a kWh. The plan uses solar for the car when that is cheaper than buying from the grid at another time.

Charging on surplus

Home battery

The app plans the home battery next to the car: charge from the grid in cheap hours when that pays off after losses and wear, save the energy for expensive hours, take the sun in between, and decide whether the battery may charge the car.

Battery

Wear: what a discharged kWh costs in battery life (price ÷ guaranteed kWh throughput, often 0.02–0.05). Charging from the grid only happens when the price difference covers losses and wear.

Report a problem

Something not working? Download the diagnostics and attach the file to a bug report on GitHub. It contains your settings, what the app found, the last decisions and log lines, so the problem can be found without asking you much. Names, trip titles, places and your notify target are removed; no passwords or tokens are in it. You can open the file to check before you share it.

Back up or move your settings

Export saves all settings of this app in one file: car, charger, grid, prices, departures, rules, solar, battery and notifications. Import puts them back, here or in another install (for example the test version). "Allow control" and the other options in Home Assistant's Configuration tab are not part of it: they stay as they are.

Home battery test

Send one battery action by hand, to check that the commands work with your battery (Settings › Battery). The plan takes over again within a minute.

Setup wizard

Walks you through Vehicle, Charger, Grid and Prices again. Your current settings are kept.

Find your price source

The app looks for integrations that provide dynamic electricity prices for today and tomorrow.

Price source

Your tariff

Enter the prices per kWh as on your energy bill, including energy tax, fees and VAT.

Costs on top of the price

Used to calculate what you really pay per kWh. Check your energy contract for these amounts.

Price forecast (optional)

Real prices are only known for today and, from about 13:00, tomorrow. With a sensor that has expected prices for the coming days, the app can wait for a cheaper day before your departure. It never charges on a forecast price: the plan is recalculated when the real prices come out.

Added to every forecast price, so the app only waits when the forecast is clearly cheaper. 0 = trust the forecast fully.

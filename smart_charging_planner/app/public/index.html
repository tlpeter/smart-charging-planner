Home Assistant helper

Use a date/time helper (and optionally a number helper for the battery level), for example to set the departure from a dashboard or an automation.

Calendar

Trips in a calendar become departures. The start of the event is the departure time. All-day events are skipped.

A target in the event ("doel: 80", "target: 90" or "85%") is used for that trip. "precondition: ja" is read and shown, but not used yet.

Charger

How the app starts and stops the charger. Found by the control check; for example choose the method your own automation used.

Not used yet: the app only starts and pauses the charger. Your load balancer keeps setting the current.

Car

The car's own charge limit and a minimum battery level.

Sets the car's limit to the goal: the Charge now level while it runs, otherwise the battery level of your next departure (for example "doel: 90" in the calendar). Only while the car is plugged in. A new limit is sent once, right away; it is only sent again if the car still shows the old value after 15 minutes (at most twice), because the car's cloud limits the number of calls.

Timing and price

While this entity is "on", the charger stays on, so heating or cooling the car uses grid power instead of the battery. For example the car's HVAC status, or your own helper. Only entities of your car and entities about preconditioning, HVAC or airco are listed.

Always on: once a planned period has started, it is finished even if a new calculation would move it, and a charger status that is briefly invalid (up to 2 minutes) is ignored.

Find your price source

The app looks for integrations that provide dynamic electricity prices for today and tomorrow.

Price source

Your tariff

Enter the prices per kWh as on your energy bill, including energy tax, fees and VAT.

Costs on top of the price

Used to calculate what you really pay per kWh. Check your energy contract for these amounts.

Price forecast (optional)

Real prices are only known for today and, from about 13:00, tomorrow. With a sensor that has expected prices for the coming days, the app can wait for a cheaper day before your departure. It never charges on a forecast price: the plan is recalculated when the real prices come out.

Added to every forecast price, so the app only waits when the forecast is clearly cheaper. 0 = trust the forecast fully.

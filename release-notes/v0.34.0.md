Stable release with a longer look ahead and better price sources for more homes and Nord Pool areas.

### New
- **Looking ahead: 7, 14 or 30 days.** Choose it above the list on Home; Plan follows the same choice. Days after the last known price are marked as an estimate. (#4)
- **Tibber with more homes, Nord Pool with more areas:** choose yours under Settings › Prices. Before, the first home or area in the answer was always used. (#16)

### Fixed
- **Nord Pool from HACS** works next to Home Assistant's own Nord Pool integration: its sensor is offered, with the right unit and VAT. (#17)
- **Charge now keeps its priority** when chargers share the connection, also while that car is below its minimum. (#35)

### Other
- Tested against Home Assistant 2026.10.4; made-up place names in tests and the changelog.

### Upgrade notes
- With more than one Tibber home or Nord Pool area, check the chosen one under Settings › Prices after updating. With only one, nothing changes.

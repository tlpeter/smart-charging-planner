Stable release with a trip log from the car's odometer, a better trip home, preconditioning and the arrival level in Looking ahead, and the look of your Home Assistant theme.

### New
- **Trip log** (Settings › Vehicle, on by default; Activity › Trips): every drive with its real km from the car's odometer, the battery used, the use per 100 km and the outside temperature, built from Home Assistant's long-term statistics, so earlier drives show at once. With enough drives the trip estimates and the cold correction use the log. **Use more in the cold** is now on by default. Only reads statistics. (#61)
- **Trip home ("Naar huis"):** the car is home at the start of the trip home plus the drive, not at the end of the appointment. Home shows a **Drive home** row and **±Back home**; Plan marks it as **Trip home**. (#62)
- **Preconditioning in the whole Looking ahead schedule:** the ❄ time in the line of each trip and on the drive itself, for 7, 14 and 30 days; only what the app will really ask for. (#64)
- **Arrival percentage stays visible** at Back home while the car is away, in the day summary and the expanded row; separate from the live battery level. (#65)
- **The look of Home Assistant:** the app uses the active Home Assistant theme for every user and every part of the app, light or dark as in Home Assistant. Only the look changed. (#63)

### Fixed
- **Preconditioning before a trip home** with only "precondition: ja" (no target or keyword) now starts. (#58)
- **On/off switches** are easy to see when off, also in the dark theme. (#59)
- **Charging stops:** with preferred operators, only their stations are used; when none fits, the trip says so. (#60)
- **Easee integration v2026.11.0:** its Equalizer limit actions are never offered as a charger current method; "Set charger dynamic limit" and the Charger enabled switch stay the recommended methods. (#57)

### Other
- The real Home Assistant Core check now runs on 2026.11.0 (and still on 2026.4.2); new unit tests for the trip log and the theme.

### Upgrade notes
- **Use more in the cold** is turned on once for cars saved before; turn it off under Settings › Vehicle. Without a chosen weather forecast the first one in Home Assistant is used; without any, the correction does nothing.
- **Keep a trip log** is on by default and only reads Home Assistant's statistics. The odometer and an outdoor temperature sensor are found by themselves, or can be chosen under Settings › Vehicle.
- The app now follows the Home Assistant theme instead of its own colours.

### Tested
For this release commit in GitHub Actions: syntax, persistence and coverage; every unit suite; the settings test for three set-ups (Renault + Easee, Skoda + Wallbox, Kia + Zaptec); the charger × battery matrix; and a real Home Assistant Core (2026.11.0 and 2026.4.2). These run against a fake Home Assistant and fake services. The changes in this release were not yet checked on real hardware by the owner at the time of release: they are fixture-tested and Core-tested only.

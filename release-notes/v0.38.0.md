Stable release with Pause until and Hand back control, a fix for the charger staying on after preconditioning, clearer departures, Battery care in the chart and trip estimates that use more battery in the cold.

### New
- **Pause until** (Home › Quick choices): no charging until a time, until the car is unplugged, or until you resume. It is sent right away and wins over the plan, the minimum, Ready Guard and preconditioning. **Hand back control** (Activity › Log › Who controls the devices): the app shows what it controls, puts back what it changed (charging current, phases, home battery settings) and then sends nothing until you press Resume control. (#33)
- **Departure not used:** on each day one source counts. When a later departure of a higher source drops an earlier one on the same day (a calendar trip at 18:00 and the weekly schedule at 07:00), Home and Plan now warn with both times. All-day events that look like a trip are listed as "Not used as a trip", and the manual explains departure priority. The planning itself is unchanged. (#25)
- **Battery care in the chart:** the part that charges above the care level is green, with a legend entry. New option "Charge the rest earlier when that saves at least" (empty = never): when charging that part at the cheapest time saves at least this amount, the app does so and raises the car's charge limit right away. (#39)
- **Use more in the cold** (Settings › Vehicle, off by default): below 15 °C a trip is estimated to use a percentage more per degree colder, with the temperature from a weather forecast in Home Assistant at the time of the trip (read only). After trips at different temperatures the app learns your car's own value. The car's range sensor is not corrected. (#56)

### Fixed
- **Preconditioning:** when the cable was unplugged while the app kept the charger on for preconditioning, the charger stayed on with no car. It is now turned off again (one retry), and the charger is kept on for preconditioning for 15 minutes at most, never after the departure time. (#55)

### Other
- New unit tests for departures and the weather forecast; the test plan lists them.

### Upgrade notes
- **Use more in the cold** is off by default. To use it, choose a weather forecast (for example the Met.no forecast) under Settings › Vehicle.
- **Charge the rest earlier when that saves at least** is empty by default: Battery care works as before, only the colour and the note on Home are new.
- **Preconditioning:** with a long preconditioning time (for example 30 minutes), the charger is now kept on for the first 15 minutes only.

### Tested
For this release commit in GitHub Actions: syntax, persistence and coverage; every unit suite; the settings test for three set-ups (Renault + Easee, Skoda + Wallbox, Kia + Zaptec); the charger × battery matrix; and a real Home Assistant Core. These run against a fake Home Assistant, a fake weather forecast and fake OSRM / Open Charge Map servers. The changes in this release were not yet checked on real hardware by the owner at the time of release: they are fixture-tested and Core-tested only.

Stable release with charging stops in all of Europe and a route map, the car's location when it is not plugged in, and sharing the connection per phase.

### New
- **Where is the car:** when the car is not plugged in, the app no longer assumes it is at home. It checks the car's own location (a device tracker), then a person who drives, then a trip home in the calendar. When none of them knows, Home says so and Looking ahead shows planned charging as "if plugged in by then". (#46)
- **Sharing the connection per phase** (more chargers): a budget for L1, L2 and L3 instead of one number. A one-phase charger only uses its own phase; without a fresh grid reading only the first charger charges, at 6 A; a load balancer can protect only some chargers; the home battery charges from the grid only with the room the chargers leave. With one charger nothing changes. (#29)
- **Charging stops in all of Europe:** every European country, more than one at a time, routes across a border, more stops on a long trip with the level on arrival and how far to charge ("Charge to at a stop"), and "Arrive at a charger with at least". (#40)
- **Charging stops settings:** countries and operators in lists that open and close, with search; operators seen in the station data on your routes can be preferred too. (#48)
- **Route map** (off by default): the route, home, the destination and the numbered stops on a map from OpenStreetMap or your own tile server, one click from Looking ahead (#47), with zoom and drag through Leaflet, shipped with the app (#54).
- **Compatibility overview:** COMPATIBILITY.md lists per integration what the app reads and steers and what was checked on real hardware, built from the tests, with a **Beta test report** issue form. (#28)

### Fixed
- **Addresses abroad:** the address search tries your own country first, then every country. (#49)
- **Charging stops planned too early:** a stop is now as late as the battery allows, at the best station (your operators, most power, most points), with the alternatives under "Or:". (#50)
- **Home closed what you opened:** a day in Looking ahead, Plan details and the plan table stay open across the reload every minute. (#51)

### Other
- The app image is built for amd64 and aarch64, the Home Assistant Core check also runs on 2026.4.2, the release is made by a workflow after green tests, and all test data is anonymous.

### Upgrade notes
- **Car location and person** (Settings › Vehicle) are empty by default. Without them, an unplugged car is "not known" instead of "at home": Looking ahead then shows charging as "if plugged in". Set the car location to get the old "at home" status back when the car is at home.
- **More chargers:** check Settings › Grid (phase current sensors, which chargers a load balancer protects) and set the phase of a one-phase charger in Settings › Charger. When its phase is not known, the app keeps room on all three phases.
- **Route map** is off by default (Plan › Charging stops on the way). Map images are only loaded from the tile server when you open a map.
- Charging stops: "Arrive at a charger with at least" starts at 10 % and "Charge to at a stop" at 80 %.

### Tested
For this release commit in GitHub Actions: syntax, persistence and coverage; every unit suite; the settings test for three set-ups (Renault + Easee, Skoda + Wallbox, Kia + Zaptec); the charger × battery matrix (10 chargers); the car integrations; and a real Home Assistant Core (2026.10.4 and 2026.4.2). These run against a fake Home Assistant and fake OSRM / Open Charge Map servers. Physical cars, chargers and batteries are only checked where the owner tested them at home; sharing per phase (#29) is fixture-tested only, and operator names are not checked against live Open Charge Map data.

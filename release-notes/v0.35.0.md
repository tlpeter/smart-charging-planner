Stable release with charging stops on the way, preconditioning before a departure, and more control over what the app sends and how prices are built.

### New
- **Charging stops on the way** (Netherlands and Belgium, off by default): when a trip needs more than the battery holds, Looking ahead suggests up to three public charging stations along the route, from Open Charge Map with your own free API key. Choose countries, preferred operators, connector, minimum power, maximum detour and the level below which a stop is suggested. (#37)
- **Preconditioning before a departure** (off by default, Settings › Rules): the app presses a button or turns on a switch of your car a set number of minutes before it leaves (default 15). From home for a calendar trip with `precondition: yes`; the next drive of a trip and a trip home follow that event; with no event for the drive home, the new setting **Preconditioning on the way back** decides. No plug needed. (#44)
- **Preconditioning for every departure source:** a Precondition tick per day in the weekly schedule, for the helper (or an on/off entity that is only read) and for a one-off departure. (#45)
- **Trip distances and privacy:** choose what may be sent to look up a trip's distance: OpenStreetMap, your own servers, offline (zones and distances you enter) or off. (#31)
- **Forecast price basis:** choose whether the price forecast is excl. VAT, incl. VAT or all-in, so an all-in forecast never gets VAT a second time. (#26)

### Fixed
- **Charging stops on the right side of the road:** the detour is the real detour by road, so a service area on the other side of a motorway is no longer suggested as if it were on the way; a stop is suggested on the way back when that fits. (#41)
- **Open Charge Map key:** a clear "Key saved" state after saving and reloading, replacement and removal on Save; the key is never sent to the page. (#42)
- **Battery Care after a short trip:** a refill within 5 percentage points of the holding level joins the planned final session; Home and Looking ahead show each session's own goal and a declined trip top-up uses the current trip estimate. (#43)

### Other
- A third full test set-up (Kia Niro EV + Zaptec Go), a documentation check on every push, and GitHub releases with notes per stable version.

### Upgrade notes
- **Trip distances:** an install from before this version keeps the OpenStreetMap services and shows a notice on Plan until you confirm or change the choice.
- **Forecast price basis:** check Settings › Prices › forecast; the basis is no longer copied from the real prices.
- Charging stops and preconditioning are **off by default**. Charging stops need an Open Charge Map API key and online trip distances. Preconditioning needs a button or switch of the car; climate entities, helpers and scripts are never controlled.

### Tested
For this release commit in GitHub Actions: syntax, persistence and coverage; every unit suite; the settings test for three set-ups (Renault + Easee, Skoda + Wallbox, Kia + Zaptec); the charger × battery matrix (10 chargers); the car integrations; and a real Home Assistant Core. These run against a fake Home Assistant and fake OSRM / Open Charge Map servers. Physical cars, chargers and batteries are only checked where the owner tested them at home; preconditioning and real Open Charge Map data are not physically verified for every car.
